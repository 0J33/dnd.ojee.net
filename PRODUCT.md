# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The same friend group that plays on mtg.ojee.net. None of them has played D&D yet, and nobody at the table knows the rules. They will play on a laptop or desktop in a voice call, with some joining from a phone.

## Product Purpose

A beginner-first Dungeons & Dragons 5e virtual tabletop. It lets a group with no experienced Dungeon Master play a real first game: build characters step by step, play on battle maps with dice, and learn through a guided adventure where the app narrates and runs the monsters. Success is a first session where the rules never stop the fun.

## Positioning

The app can be the Dungeon Master. Guided mode runs "The Cellar of the Gilded Flagon" end to end, teaching one mechanic per scene, and every number on a character sheet is derived and click-to-roll, so no one has to know the maths.

## Operating Context

- A session starts with someone creating a campaign in DM mode (they run it) or Guided mode (the app runs it) and sharing the 6-character code or invite link.
- Players build a hero in the builder or grab a ready-made one, then join the table.
- Everyone talks in a voice call; the map, dice, sheets and chat carry the game.
- Players move between dnd.ojee.net and its sibling mtg.ojee.net with one account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Character builder with 22 races and 108 subclasses, interactive sheets, level-up, spells; battle maps with fog, drawing and a map builder; DM tools with the SRD monster library; guided adventure; persistent campaigns.
- Rules content is open-licensed only: SRD 5.1 and 5.2 (CC-BY-4.0) and OGL 1.0a books via Open5e (Tome of Heroes, Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting, Open5e Originals). Nothing from non-SRD Wizards of the Coast books.
- The licenses and credits screen (sources, OGL text, Section 15) must remain.
- Accounts are shared with mtg.ojee.net: one username and password, and signing in or out on either site applies to both (decided 2026-09-22).
- "Compatible with fifth edition"; an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast; no WotC trademarks or logos.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: dnd.ojee.net, the sibling of mtg.ojee.net.
- Every hero has a drawn likeness derived from their race, class and name, used everywhere a character appears (sheet, lobby, party list, map token).
- Icons come from the one generated Material Symbols set shared across the ojee.net sites.

## Evidence on Hand

- SRD and open-licensed rules data (`server/data/`), six pregenerated heroes, the tutorial script, the rules guide.
- The group has not played a session yet: no play history, testimonials or stats exist, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Nobody at the table needs to know the rules.
- Every number is derived and every stat is a button.
- A character is recognisable anywhere it appears.
- Open content only, credited properly.
